About MCOA

Our Work & Mission

MCOA Thane-Bhiwandi Zone actively works for the benefit and professional development of coaching class owners.

Foundation

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the foremost platform for coaching class owners in Maharashtra, upholding the highest standards of quality in education and creating a unified, professional fraternity that serves students and society.

Our Mission

To represent, support, and empower coaching class owners through advocacy, networking, training, and collective action — for the benefit of students and the wider education community of Thane-Bhiwandi Zone.

What We Do

Key Activities

From academic initiatives to technology adoption, MCOA serves its members across eight key areas.

Academic Initiatives

Driving academic excellence through quality standards and student-centric programmes across the zone.

Teacher Training

Workshops and training to help teachers grow professionally and stay current with modern education methods.

Seminars & Workshops

Regular knowledge-sharing events on education trends, regulations, and best practices.

Prelim Examinations

Organising preliminary examinations to give students an edge and benchmark preparation quality.

Professional Development

Structured growth programmes and peer learning to build the professional identity of class owners.

Member Networking

A strong community where class owners collaborate, refer, and support each other across the region.

Representation

Representing coaching class owners before education authorities and policy bodies where needed.

Technology Initiatives

Supporting members in adopting educational technology to modernise teaching and administration.
